- New browser extension for Chrome, Edge, Brave and Firefox. A Lockbox button in sign-in boxes fills your login, and the toolbar button lists the logins for the site you're on.
- It offers to save new logins and changed passwords after you sign in, and makes a strong password for you on sign-up pages.
- Look-alike sites get nothing: github.com.evil.net is not GitHub. Filling a login on a different site always asks first.
- The extension only talks to Lockbox on your computer, never the internet. Turn it on in Settings → Browser extension, then click Connect in your browser and allow it in Lockbox. The guide on GitHub (docs/BROWSER.md) shows how to add it.
- Ctrl+Shift+L in your browser fills the login for the site you're on.
- Protected items still ask for your master password before they fill.
